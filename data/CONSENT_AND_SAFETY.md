# Participant Consent & Safety Note — StanceSense-RT

**What we record:** short front-on video clips of you (a) standing neutrally,
(b) seated rotating your hips to end-range, and (c) performing bodyweight squats.
We also note your approximate height and a goniometer reading of your hip rotation.

**What we do NOT record:** your name, contact details, address, or any medical
history. Subjects are referenced only by an anonymous ID (S01, S02, ...).

**Use of data:** clips are used solely to build and validate a student research
project (CSE1905 TARP). They are stored locally, not uploaded to any cloud, and
are deleted on request.

**Safety:** all hip rotations are performed **gently to comfortable end-range
only — never forced**. Stop immediately if you feel pinching or pain. Squats are
bodyweight only. Participation is voluntary and you may withdraw at any time.
